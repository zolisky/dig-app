Answers to common questions about Daily Income Goal, plus how to get in touch.

Where is my data?

Your activities, daily entries, prices, and goals are stored only on your iPhone. The app does not use an account or a server, and the developer cannot see your data. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I back up my data?

On the main screen, tap the ••• button and choose Export Data (CSV). The app creates a CSV backup and opens the iOS share sheet, so you can save the file wherever you like — for example in the Files app, iCloud Drive, or another app. Export only happens when you choose it, and you control where the file goes.

How do I restore a backup?

On the main screen, tap the ••• button and choose Import Data (CSV). Pick a CSV backup you previously exported. The app will show a summary and ask you to confirm. Importing replaces the data currently in the app. A copy of your current data is kept on the device first, in case you need it.

What if I delete the app?

If you delete the app without first exporting a backup, the data stored on your device is lost and cannot be recovered. Export a CSV backup before you delete the app, switch phones, or reset your device, and keep that file somewhere safe.

How do I get help?

Please open an issue on the Daily Income Goal support GitHub Issues page. Describe what you were doing and what went wrong, and include your iOS version if you can.

Prefer email? Write to rawcode@icloud.com.

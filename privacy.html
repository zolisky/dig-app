Privacy Policy

Daily Income Goal stores your data on your device and does not collect it.

Home Privacy Policy Support

Effective date: November 14, 2026

This policy describes how the Daily Income Goal iPhone app (“the app”) handles information. The short version: the app keeps your data on your device and does not collect it.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal data. The app has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party software development kits (SDKs). We do not sell, share, or transfer your information because we never receive it.

Data stored on your device

The activities you add, daily entries and counts, prices, and goals are stored only on your iPhone, in local storage on the device. That data stays on the device unless you choose to export it.

Export and import

You can export a CSV backup of your data and import a backup you previously saved. Export and import happen only when you choose them. When you export, iOS shows the share sheet so you decide where the file goes, such as the Files app, iCloud Drive, or another app you pick. We do not receive a copy of the file.

iCloud

You can optionally turn on iCloud Sync in the app to keep your data in sync across your devices. It is off by default. When it is on, your data is stored in your own private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. The developer cannot see or access it. You can turn sync off at any time, and you can manage or delete iCloud data in your device's iCloud settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, please open an issue on the Daily Income Goal support GitHub Issues page.

You can also email rawcode@icloud.com.
